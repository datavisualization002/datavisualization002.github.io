## Notes
These figures could be used in the [Demographics session](https://university-at-albany-youth-justice-institute.foleon.com/yji/youth-in-new-york-state-2024/demographics) in [Youth Snapshots](https://university-at-albany-youth-justice-institute.foleon.com/yji/youth-in-new-york-state-2024/). Current version uses screenshots linked to [tableau](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/nys.youth.justice.institute/viz/TotalYouthinNewYorkStatebyCounty/Dashboard1). In this way, the figure could be interactive on the Youth Snapshot directly. This figure can also match Y-FACTS [color scheme](/docs/color-scheme/).
